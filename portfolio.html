FAQ

Common Questions

01 How do lessons work?

Sessions are 60 or 90 minutes, conducted either in-person (London) or online via an interactive whiteboard. Each session begins with a brief review, moves through new material, and ends with targeted practice.

02 Do you teach online?

Yes — online sessions are fully interactive using a shared digital whiteboard. Many students prefer this format for its flexibility. Online and in-person sessions are equally effective.

03 Which age groups do you teach?

Lower Levels, GCSE (Years 10–11) and A-Level / IB (Years 12–13). I also work with university students on foundational maths and physics modules.

04 How long are sessions?

Standard sessions are 60 minutes. Extended sessions of 90 minutes are available and recommended for exam-intensive periods. Packages of 4, 8, or 12 sessions are also available.

05 Do you provide homework?

Yes — targeted practice problems are set after every session. These are calibrated to reinforce exactly what was covered. Marked and returned before the next session.

06 What is your availability?

Intake is intentionally selective to maintain quality. Weekdays and weekends. Spaces are limited — early booking is recommended, especially before exam season.
